# Future Quest Ideas

This file holds quest throughlines created during world-building passes. Keep entries high-level: location, faction motive, player action, payoff, and why the idea belongs.

Related context: ./docs/world-building.md and ./docs/world-travel.md.

## Active Seeds

### Rootway Watering Hole

Location: Ashwood edge between Oakhaven and the deeper wilderness.

Factions: Sylvan Concord and Verdant Outcasts.

Player action: Track prey movement to a shared watering hole, protect Oakhaven gatherers, then decide whether to mark it as a protected grove or leave it as a hunter-proving ground.

Payoff: Opens a low-footprint forest route, teaches predator ecology, and gives wolf or bramble pressure a natural reason to gather.

WHY: A sparse nature section should not be filled with random props. A water source naturally attracts prey, hunters, predators, scouts, and border conflict.

### Gatherer Survey Routes

Location: Common material pockets across each faction zone, with uncommon and rare nodes on route edges.

Factions: All local factions through their owned resources and trade pressure.

Player action: Follow map pins through common nodes first, then mark uncommon finds for a faction quartermaster or rival buyer.

Payoff: Turns gathering into zone learning, gives early players visible short errands, and makes rare materials feel discovered rather than hidden by noise.

WHY: A dense material economy needs readable field routes. Common nodes teach where a zone's resources live, while uncommon and rare pins create small exploration goals.

### Quarry Overflow Camp

Location: Road between Ironcrag and Deep Fissure.

Factions: Vulcan Guild, with Dust Syndicate trade pressure.

Player action: Move surplus miners and supplies out of the guild hall into a road camp, secure the ore-cart stop, and choose whether to report missing powder as theft or safety failure.

Payoff: Spreads town density into a readable work route, creates a labor-protection task, and sets up Sapper-Goblin or Rust-Biter pressure.

WHY: If Ironcrag gets too dense, labor should move to where extraction happens instead of crowding the civic core.

### Smuggler's Sinkhole

Location: Port Marrow to Canaan Desert route edge.

Factions: Free Cove, Dust Syndicate, and Archon Order.

Player action: Buy or steal a passkey, escort contraband through an underground sinkhole, and evade Aetherian inspection.

Payoff: Turns travel friction into a faction choice and gives Port Marrow plus the Sunken Bazaar a shared route identity.

WHY: Free movement and contract trade both benefit from a route that exists because law pressure makes direct travel costly.

### Wind Library Thermal

Location: High-Reach Peaks cliff path.

Factions: Ascetics of Sky.

Player action: Follow eagle thermal patterns, recover wind-marked archive scraps, and prove movement discipline before using a glider aery.

Payoff: Makes altitude mastery visible before adding faster travel and connects creature behavior to route unlocks.

WHY: High-Reach should feel alive through wind, birds, and controlled movement rather than generic mountain ruins.

### Failed Rift Crossing

Location: Void Rift outer chunks.

Factions: Nihil Sect and any faction resisting Void travel.

Player action: Investigate a broken portal waymark, recover remains or cargo from a failed crossing, and decide whether to stabilize the route or exploit its corruption.

Payoff: Explains sparse dangerous space, previews premium Void travel costs, and creates a reason for shelters, warning signs, and rift predators.

WHY: Empty Void space can feel intentional when it reads as lethal distance around unstable transit.

### Gate Readback Throughlines

Location: Oakhaven soft root and mill routes, Overgrown Thicket, Ironcrag work-order and forge yards, Aetherian archive, courtyard drill, and Void-border wards, Port Marrow oath docks, reef cuts, and black current, Bazaar open ledger, vault, and Rift price stones, Obsidian sample and bargain sites, far Void route, Ashwood boundary, pack, and treant wake, and High-Reach first-breath, thermal, and mirror lines.

Factions: Sylvan Concord, Vulcan Guild, Archon Order, Free Cove, Dust Syndicate, Nihil Sect, Verdant Outcasts, and Ascetics of Sky.

Player action: Prove one local mastery gate before the route or faction payoff expands: neutral-impact gathering, clean mill protection, thicket diplomacy, labor logistics, forge ecology management, archive handling, formation discipline, reef scouting, oath accounting, black-current responsibility, open-ledger contract work, asset-safe ruin clearing, market stabilization, controlled Void exposure, life-force bargaining, radiant border prep, boundary reading, pack-law route control, rift-pressure survival, treant wake control, first-breath preparation, eagle-thermal discipline, or mirror self-control.

Payoff: Daily faction work can state what proof the player earned and what future route, recipe, travel, or mastery beat that proof supports.

WHY: The world already has many authored places. The next progression gain is making each step say why the current task unlocks a new kind of trust instead of feeling like another chore.

### First-Hour Rumor Ledger

Location: Oakhaven safe ring, rootway border, and first road toward the Overgrown Thicket.

Factions: Sylvan Concord first, with light previews of Vulcan Guild, Free Cove, Ascetics of Sky, and Nihil Sect.

Player action: Inspect visible but locked future objects near safe play: a rootway marker, a forge shipment note, a dock-route map, a wind-step rumor, and a Void warning. Each clue adds one field-journal entry that names what looks cool, why it is locked, and which local proof starts the unlock path.

Payoff: New players can start playing immediately, then ask "how do I unlock that?" through world evidence instead of tutorial screens. Experienced players can ignore the rumors and keep moving.

WHY: Streamlined onboarding should not hide the world's depth or force a systems lecture. Rumors let future travel, crafting, faction, and movement goals become visible curiosity before they become active menus.

### First Arms Proving Contract

Location: Oakhaven practice yard, with follow-up vouchers pointing to the first safe route toward Ironcrag, Port Marrow, Aetheria, the Sunken Bazaar, High-Reach, the Verdant Outcasts, and the Obsidian Sanctum.

Factions: Sylvan Concord sponsors the first contract; every city can later fund one paid lesson that introduces its local weapon, material, buff, status, crafting, or travel identity.

Player action: Enter an optional paid proving contract, try loaner weapons against safe demo enemies, choose one reward weapon, then accept follow-up contracts for statuses, gathering, crafting, buffs, city identity, and travel options.

Payoff: Gives uncertain players a high-reward path into combat feel and starter economy while preserving immediate free play for players who skip tutorials.

WHY: A weaponless sampler can make "what do I want to become?" playable instead of a class-menu choice, but it only works if it is opt-in, well-paid, and written back into local routes rather than becoming a mandatory lecture.

### Enemy Camp Frequency Chain

Location: Major travel lanes from the forest border through the far Void route.

Factions: Local faction per camp, with Nihil Sect pressure dominating the far eastern chain.

Player action: Clear or study each camp type as a local ecology problem: wolf den, bramble ambush, sapper crater, rat cache, contraband wreck, crusader relic, sentinel drill, eagle watch, jackal bones, leech sink, warning bivouac, rift lamp, gargoyle gate, violet crystal, gravity leech cairn, rot bloom, failed bones, crawler burrow, amalgam scar, and oracle tower.

Payoff: Travel stays readable and threatening before several screens pass without a landmark. Future contracts can ask for camp-specific proof instead of generic kills.

WHY: Empty distance was making the expanded map feel unfinished. Enemy camps give sparse lanes visual anchors, combat congregation, and quest handles without turning every gap into another town.

## Recent Density Pass Seeds

### Five-Pass Progression Loop

These five passes bridge visible world dressing into player progression:

- Rootway forager hut: escort gatherers from the watering hole toward a safer hut, then decide whether to reveal the path to Oakhaven or leave it hidden from Ironcrag scouts.
- Glider aery edge: prove wind discipline across the far aery markers before a trusted Ascetic allows glider travel.
- Rot-safe shelter rib: recover a failed traveler's supplies from a shelter pocket and decide whether to reinforce it for other factions.
- Route-pull gravity cairn: read cairn drift and relay scars to learn which Void route bends are safe, unstable, or deliberately cultivated.
- Bloom spread warning: collect unstable samples near the third crossing to learn whether the Nihil Sect is warning travelers away or baiting them deeper.

WHY: The five-pass loop turns density scan results into progression gates. Dense towns push short-session errands outward; sparse Void space becomes staged mastery distance with visible shelter, route-pull, and corruption evidence.

### Ten-Pass Distribution Loop

These are the ten recent distribution passes to preserve as future quest hooks:

- Oakhaven living fence: protect the root gate and decide where gentle forest safety ends.
- Ironcrag blast wall: inspect a slag-stone barrier before opening a deeper ore route.
- Aetheria toll wall: pass a radiant inspection without losing contraband or pilgrims.
- Port Marrow oath gate: move salvage through a guarded dock border before the oath breaks.
- Sunken Bazaar contract fence: settle a toll dispute before a caravan turns violent.
- Ashwood blood fence: read pack warnings and choose between hunter law and town safety.
- High-Reach wind trial gate: prove discipline at the low-step barrier before taking the cliff path.
- Obsidian pressure wall: stabilize gravity stones before rot crosses the Sanctum border.
- Rootway watering hole: follow gatherables and safe-road markers into a contested border route.
- Far Rift support chain: use waymarks, shelters, eye-growth samples, wrecks, rocks, and water hazards to make the long Void lane feel navigated and lethal.

### Rootway Border Sign - Chunk 0

Location: Ashwood and crag seam near Oakhaven, Overgrown Thicket, and Deep Fissure.

Factions: Sylvan Concord, Verdant Outcasts, and Vulcan Guild.

Player action: Follow the east rootway marker and living ward to find where forest scouts are redirecting travelers away from crowded town paths.

Payoff: Creates a border route quest that can teach forest movement, wolf trail reading, and the difference between Oakhaven protection and Outcast territory claims.

WHY: Dense Ashwood and crag reads had too many services competing inside the first chunk. Moving scout and ward reads outward lets the town core stay readable while making the border feel patrolled.

### Void-Edge Debt Scale - Chunk 3

Location: Sunken Spire approach between the Bazaar and the Void Rift.

Factions: Dust Syndicate and Nihil Sect.

Player action: Inspect the outer vault seal and debt scale, then decide whether to honor a contract checkpoint or slip toward Void corruption without settlement protection.

Payoff: Turns the dense Bazaar and High-Reach seam into a clearer approach route and sets up contract, relic appraisal, or smuggling pressure before the Void.

WHY: Bazaar density should not all sit inside the market. Debt enforcement naturally follows travelers to vault edges, especially where valuable relic routes approach the Rift.

### Far Rift Crossing - Chunk 6

Location: Far eastern Void Rift beyond the Obsidian Sanctum.

Factions: Nihil Sect and any faction trying to resist unsafe Void transit.

Player action: Follow a faint waymark to a failed crossing pocket, recover proof of what went wrong, and choose whether to stabilize the route or leave it as a warning.

Payoff: Gives sparse far-rift space a visible travel reason and can later support Void route costs, rescue tasks, rot shelter unlocks, or portal sabotage.

WHY: A completely sparse Void chunk reads unfinished. A small waymark, wreck, and rift bloom make emptiness feel hostile and intentional without adding a full new settlement.

### East Moss Border - Chunk 0

Location: Eastern Oakhaven border toward the Overgrown Thicket route.

Factions: Sylvan Concord and Verdant Outcasts.

Player action: Follow the moss border and relocated scout blind to find which forest crossings Oakhaven still monitors and which ones the Outcasts have claimed.

Payoff: Gives the starter forest a second route read without crowding the town center, and can later support patrol, lost scout, or rootway-access tasks.

WHY: The starter chunk was over-dense. Border vegetation and scout infrastructure belong on the route edge because patrol work happens where town safety ends.

### Eastern Cliff Step - Chunk 3

Location: High-Reach and Canaan seam, east of the main mountain court.

Factions: Ascetics of Sky and Dust Syndicate.

Player action: Use the displaced cliff-step marker to test wind discipline before crossing into contract-controlled desert routes.

Payoff: Turns a crowded High-Reach/Bazaar seam into a clearer approach line and gives future glider or wind-route quests a visible first step.

WHY: A mountain movement read should not all sit in the temple cluster. A cliff step belongs on the route edge where discipline becomes travel.

### Second Rift Waymark - Chunk 7

Location: Deeper Void Rift beyond the first failed crossing pocket.

Factions: Nihil Sect.

Player action: Trace the second waymark through a gravity-stone pocket and recover signs of travelers pulled off course.

Payoff: Creates a middle stretch for a future Void route chain, letting travel danger escalate before the final crossing.

WHY: Sparse Void space should read as distance under pressure. A second waymark and wreck make the emptiness feel navigated and dangerous.

### Third Rift Warning - Chunk 8

Location: Far Void Rift, beyond stable Obsidian Sanctum sightlines.

Factions: Nihil Sect and any faction attempting rescue or sabotage.

Player action: Compare the third warning sign with bloom growth to determine whether the route is worsening or being cultivated.

Payoff: Sets up rescue, sabotage, or route-stabilization choices without adding a full settlement.

WHY: A warning sign plus rift bloom makes a sparse chunk tell a cause-and-effect story: someone marked the danger, then the Void kept growing.

### Last Rift Debris - Chunk 9

Location: Last eastern Void Rift edge.

Factions: Nihil Sect.

Player action: Reach the final debris field, recover unstable crystal fragments, and decide whether to turn back or pay the full cost of Void travel.

Payoff: Gives the far map edge a clear endpoint for future premium transit, salvage, or rot-resistance tasks.

WHY: The far edge of the map should not feel like unfilled canvas. Debris, warning, and crystal reads make it feel like the last survivable stop before the route fails.
