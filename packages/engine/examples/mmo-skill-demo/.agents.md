# MMO Skill Demo guidance

The [README](README.md) owns the example boundary and current claim limits.
Use the local player-skill-update, player-progression, or world-building skill
only for its corresponding ability, progression, or world-content task.

## Product constraints

- Controller play must reach and reverse ordinary combat, HUD, panel, travel,
  inventory, gear, quest, market and dialog actions without a mouse. Preserve
  visible focus, stable order, input-mode prompts and a digital path requiring
  no simultaneous presses. The established mapping is stick/D-pad focus, X
  confirm, Circle back, shoulders tabs/pages; right stick aims during gameplay.
  Deadzone is persisted and defaults to 5%. Configurable bindings must drive
  hints, tutorials, hotbar labels and control maps.
- Change authored base values for permanent numeric tuning; runtime multipliers
  belong to live buffs or temporary modifiers.
- Existing zone constraints remain until new zones actually run. Expansion data
  must distinguish implemented terrain, quests, recipe choice and assets from
  future intent. Build reputation/material loops through existing inventory,
  quests, rewards, vendors and crafting before adding separate systems.

## Content routes

- World, faction, ecology or landmark changes: update the affected section of
  [world building](docs/world-building.md) with real zone IDs, player meaning and
  current limitations. Runtime behavior is canonical; do not claim unimplemented
  art or quest chains. Expose faction data through existing catalog/UI surfaces.
- Routes, costs, gates or travel UI: update [world travel](docs/world-travel.md).
- Visual direction: use approved existing areas and [art notes](assets/world/NOTES.md)
  before inventing a style. Preserve source provenance in `assets/source/craftpix/`.
  If those examples cannot settle a material taste decision, present concrete
  alternatives. Large terrain/landmark passes use `bun.cmd run screenshot:world`
  to check dominant silhouettes, prop variety and readable services/danger.
