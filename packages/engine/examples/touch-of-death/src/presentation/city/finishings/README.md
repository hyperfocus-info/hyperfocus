# Civilian and institutional finishes

`authorCityFinishes({ declaration, combat })` follows structural authoring and the
semantic palette. The October 1 empty-city captures exposed a specific art gap:
the five districts stood on one uninterrupted asphalt sheet, while blank surfaces
made ordinary buildings read as a showroom of obstacle modules.

Five low-contrast paving aprons frame the dark road network. Sparse expansion
cuts, a repaired utility trench, delivery tracks and boarding thresholds
describe uses instead of uniformly distributing debris. The ground layers have
explicit separation above the street; they leave both underground mouths open.
They do not add physics or change fighter support height.

Mounted details cluster at real locations: produce in fitted market trays,
painted stall prices, serving drawers and cloth, apartment notices, warm porch
windows and curtains, balcony laundry, cargo labels, divided waiting seats,
timetables and construction drawings. These are fittings, not highlighted fake
pickup props. Repeated repair panels expose the same maintenance authority across
districts. Three identical full-height human studies appear on rear observation
monitors with hollow scan torsos and horizontal scan lines through three
translucent panes; the fourth pane stays opaque. These
forms suggest the replication program from the normal roof camera.

The next normal-camera review exposed empty, interchangeable roof stations.
`roof-stations.ts` gives them visible working identities: a construction plan and
offcuts, market delivery case, exercise bench with weight plates and a towel,
wheeled depot service cart, and utility rack. Short-prop support lanes remain
clear. Ordinary furniture labels use muted public paint; an unstocked cabinet
must not advertise a bright orange interaction. The depot waiting island is now
seat-height, with divided seats and backrests. Utility pressure instruments belong
to its actual street-facing switchgear.

`oversight.ts` places a dark, brass-marked observation bay above the blue study
floor, with remote operator silhouettes behind consoles. This suggests a
privileged authority above the utilitarian testing operation without adding AI
or exposition text. Fifty-six distant drain bars, seams and tiny inspection
marks were removed to fund these camera-visible forms within the same pool.

The pool has a hard 256-entity authoring budget and no animation or physics.
Existing structural district caps, weapon stock, combat rules and destruction
definitions remain unchanged. Mounted pieces keep their host's local offset in
all three structural arrangements and follow its camera fade. A destructible
host also hides its attached finish when broken. Compact generation scales the
same portable pieces through the existing city finish pass.

`author.test.ts` checks the bounded pool, open underground mouths, three seeds of
host alignment and exact repeated-authoring parity. These checks establish the
portable assembly contract; normal-camera gameplay and moving footage remain the
visual acceptance, and bounded counts alone do not prove frame-time performance.
