# Licensing

The operative [Hyperfocus Source-Available Game License 2.0](../LICENSE) applies to all first-party material in this repository. Its definition of `Software` expressly includes all source code, object code, documentation, applications, packages, tools, scripts, assets, and other first-party materials contained in or distributed from the repository. That scope includes the engine, engine editor, AI development harness, shared packages, and all other first-party code.

Materials expressly identified as third-party or governed by separate license terms are excluded from `Software` and remain under their respective terms. Third-party notices are collected in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), with example-specific notices retained beside their assets.

The license permits viewing and modification, enumerated non-commercial uses, commercial Licensed Games under its threshold and royalty rules, and no-charge redistribution of the Software under the same terms. Including a component in the repository does not create a general right to sell that component: Section 5's restrictions on monetized Software and Modifications continue to apply. The license text controls over this summary.

Two publication details remain unsettled:

- `Licensor` is still a generic placeholder rather than the legal person or entity that owns the copyright.
- Royalty reporting and payment contact details are not published.

Those values require an owner decision and legal review; they have not been invented here. The custom license is not OSI-approved and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial launch.
