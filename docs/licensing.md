# Licensing

The imported engine supplied the operative Hyperfocus Source-Available Game License 2.0, and that license is now present at the monorepo root. It permits viewing, modification, non-commercial use, commercial games under its threshold/royalty rules, and no-charge redistribution of the engine under the same terms. The license text controls over this summary.

The current text defines the licensed “Software” as the “Hyperfocus engine.” That wording does not unambiguously extend the custom terms to the harness, applications, and shared packages merely because the file is at the repository root. The repository is therefore not ready to claim one clearly scoped source-available license for every first-party component until the owner deliberately broadens that definition or supplies package-specific license terms. No legal language has been silently rewritten during this migration.

Two publication details are not settled in the imported material:

- `Licensor` is still a generic placeholder rather than the legal person or entity that owns the copyright.
- royalty reporting and payment contact details are not published.

Those values require an owner decision and legal review; they have not been invented here. The custom license is not OSI-approved and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial launch.

Third-party notices are collected in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), with example-specific notices retained beside their assets.
